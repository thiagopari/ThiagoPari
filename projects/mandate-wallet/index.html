Mandate Wallet: Signed Spending Limits for AI Agents

PayPal AI Hackathon, Nov 2026

PythonClaudePayPal Vault v3 + Orders v2Ed25519Render
Live demo on PayPal sandbox

The Idea

An AI shopping agent (Claude) spends from the owner’s real PayPal wallet, but only inside a mandate the owner signed.

How a Purchase Gets Through

  1. The owner signs a mandate. In the demo: $150 budget, $60 per purchase, groceries and office supplies, 7 days.
  2. Claude shops in chat, and every purchase goes through a gate written in plain code: 13 checks before any PayPal vault charge.
  3. Over the cap, the purchase pauses for the owner: a $93 order waited for approval. Outside the mandate, it is blocked.
  4. Each allowed purchase ends in a signed receipt; the wallet, the agent and the gate each hold an Ed25519 key.
