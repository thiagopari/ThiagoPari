Experience

Robotics Engineer Co-op, Underwater Perception & 3D Mapping — Archenaux Robotics

Somerville, MA · May – Sep 2026

Owned the 3D mapping architecture on a 5–7 person team building an underwater robot that cleans and inspects ship hulls. Ruled out visual SLAM early (median 51 ORB keypoints per frame on recorded camera data) and moved the design to sonar + IMU.

  • Traced sonar SLAM failure on real pool data (scan matching recovered only 0–6% of along-track motion) to vertical-mount bias, not noise; recommended a horizontal mount, which reached 0.2–0.3 m ATE in a pool simulation at slow survey speed
  • Wrote ROS 2 drivers for the Ping360 sonar and VN-100 IMU, which a teammate integrated into the vehicle firmware; traced slow sonar sweeps to a serial-transfer bottleneck; per-beam time fell ~4× (133 → 32 ms)
  • Audited 8 pool recordings and found 4 data defects, including a frame bug (FRD vs. FLU) in my own IMU driver that reversed lap headings
  • Trained a real-time U-Net hull-inspection segmenter (0.50 test mIoU over 7 classes; 247 FPS model-only at 512 px on a laptop GPU) after rejecting public datasets that labeled red paint as rust
  • Built an Isaac Sim Replicator synthetic-data pipeline and a sonar survey simulator calibrated from recorded rosbags
