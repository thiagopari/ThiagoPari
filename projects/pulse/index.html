The Idea

Hands-only CPR practice with an AI 911 dispatcher, for Snap Spectacles. Practice the rhythm, so it’s there when it counts.

How a Round Works

  1. Check. “Breathing normally?” Yes or No.
  2. Press. A ring pulses at 110 per minute, and the live rate reads FASTER (under 100), GOOD (100–120) or SLOWER (over 120).
  3. Ask. A button asks the dispatcher, for example “Should I stop?”. The reply is a short LLM line through Snap’s Remote Service Gateway, or a scripted line if the call fails or takes over 3 s.
  4. Done. 30 good presses, then Again.
